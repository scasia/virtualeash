# virtualeash 🐾

> A leash for your iPhone!

---

## ✨ Features

- Ultra-Wideband (UWB) distance measuring app, made to be a leash. Now, your puppy can be on a leash without getting weird looks!
- Clicker
- *(to do)* Integration with BLE toys, like Lovense / Love Spouse, to punish your sub better
- *(maybe)* AirTag integration (if not possible, maybe a custom hardware product)

---

## 📱 Requirements

- **iOS 16.0+** (iOS 17+ recommended for Dynamic Island animations)
- **Two UWB-equipped iOS devices**:
  - iPhone 11 or later (excluding iPhone SE 2nd/3rd gen)

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Download the `.ipa`
Grab the latest `.ipa` file from the **[Releases](https://github.com/your-username/virtualeash/releases)** page.

### 2. Sideload onto your devices
Sideload the `.ipa` onto both iPhones using your preferred sideloading tool:
- **[Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/)** (macOS & Windows)
- **[AltStore](https://altstore.io/)**
- **[Plume](https://github.com/0xPlume/Plume)** (formerly Feather / Plume Impactor)

### 3. Connect them together!
Open the app on both devices, assign roles (Dom & Sub), and pair!
